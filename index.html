Chase for Fortune - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

In short: we don't ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress stays on your phone. Free players see ads from Google AdMob, which can use your device's advertising ID. Payments for Fortune Premium and coin packs are handled by Google Play, and we never see your card details.

This policy explains how Fortune Games ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Chase for Fortune (the "Game") for Android.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The Game has no sign-up, login, chat or social features, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

We do not run servers that receive data from the Game, and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress on your phone only: story chapters, coins, rides, outfits, mansion and yacht upgrades, missions, the daily gift and your settings (such as sound). It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free players may see ads: short videos they choose to watch for a reward, and an ad between some runs. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address (which can show your approximate location),
  • device information such as model, operating system and language,
  • how you interact with ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped).

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. Anywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ads personalisation in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads on some phones).

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Fortune Premium players do not see ads.

4. In-app purchases

Fortune Premium and coin packs are bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. The Game only receives a confirmation that a purchase was made, so it can unlock what you bought. We never see or store your payment details.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with anyone. The only third parties involved are Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play (purchases), as described above.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Game data: delete it any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage.
  • Ads: reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your phone's settings. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can also change your consent inside the Game.
  • Questions or requests: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have rights to access or delete information about you. Because we hold none, most requests concern Google's ad data, which you can manage at My Ad Center. You can still contact us at any time.

8. Security

Your progress never leaves your phone through us. Ad and purchase data travels to Google over encrypted connections.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the Game handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the Game.
